# Interaction icon concept v4

Built-in image generation. Concept board with seven icons and two action-panel examples; not an individual transparent sprite pack. Login-screen prop rendering is the primary reference.

Use case: ui-mockup.
Create one polished 1536x1024 landscape art-direction sheet for SEVEN matching pirate co-op game interaction icons, plus compact examples of the bottom-right action HUD. Input 1 is the authoritative art style reference: the actual LOGIN SCREEN. Match how its wooden props, fabric sails, iron fittings, lantern and toy-like characters are painted. Input 2 shows the existing gameplay HUD and is only a layout and palette reference.

Art direction: approachable casual maritime adventure, rounded compact hand-painted 2.5D game props. Warm honey-brown wood, matte aged brass joins, off-white canvas, charcoal iron, natural turquoise water. Soft broad two-tone shading, modest soft highlights, slightly bevelled tactile edges. Strong readable silhouettes with selective material detail. Medium detail: crafted game props, neither flat geometric pictograms nor luxurious ornate photoreal treasures. NO jewel centers, NO anchor decals on every object, NO shiny golden cannon barrels, NO white plastic wood, NO excessively intricate grain, NO neon blues, NO heavy metallic medallion backgrounds.

Sheet: dark muted navy background, spacious editorial arrangement, top header exact Korean "항해 상호작용". Seven icons in an evenly spaced grid (four upper, three lower), each icon large and unobscured with small cream Korean label under it. Icons have no individual circular frame. Same optical weight, lighting from upper left, perspective and finish throughout.
1 "조타": authentic warm wooden ship wheel with six to eight rounded handles, small aged brass central hub; clear gaps between spokes; NO check mark.
2 "돛 조절": cream canvas sail billowing softly on a short warm wood mast, simple rope loop, restrained pale blue seam accent. Distinct sail silhouette, NOT whole sailboat.
3 "대포": short chunky dark iron cannon with unmistakable hollow barrel mouth, warm wood carriage and two wheels, small aged brass pivot; no gold filigree.
4 "선체 수리": compact carpenter hammer with charcoal metal head and rounded wooden handle, diagonal, a small repair board behind it. Hammer dominates.
5 "포탄": one plain round dark iron cannonball, broad soft reflection and restrained rim light. NO fuse, NO bomb, NO cartoon face.
6 "수리 자재": two or three warm wooden boards tied together with cream rope, two visible nail heads, board grain in just a few large strokes.
7 "물 양동이": small wooden stave bucket with dark metal hoops and rope handle, turquoise water visibly inside with one small spill, no face.

Bottom strip: two small in-game action card mockups side-by-side at realistic HUD scale (roughly 320x100 pixels), using these exact icons: cannonball with Korean title "포탄 집기" and keycap "Space"; hammer with title "선체 수리" and keycap "K". Compact navy translucent-feeling rounded rectangle, very restrained thin brass edging, icon at left, cream text at right, small ivory keycap below. These cards should feel like the login panel's younger simpler siblings. Clearly separate from the main seven-icon grid. No explanation paragraphs, no color swatches, no watermark.
The final output is one cohesive concept board, not seven separate sheets. Korean labels exact and legible.
